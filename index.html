Questions

Find answers about our treatments, process, and what to expect.

What happens during consultation?

We start with a thorough skin assessment and discuss your goals before recommending any treatment.

How long until I see results?

Most clients notice changes within days, though full results develop over weeks as your skin responds to treatment.

Is there downtime involved?

Recovery time varies by treatment, but most allow you to return to daily activities immediately or within a day.

Can I combine treatments?

Yes, combining treatments often produces better results when done strategically and safely.
